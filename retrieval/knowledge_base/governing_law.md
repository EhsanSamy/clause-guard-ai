# Governing Law Playbook

## Fair Position

The agreement should specify a clear and commercially reasonable governing law
and, where appropriate, a corresponding forum or dispute-resolution mechanism.

## Preferred Position

- Governing law should be clearly identified.
- The selected jurisdiction should be commercially reasonable for the parties.
- Forum or dispute-resolution provisions should be clear.
- The agreement should avoid ambiguous or impractical jurisdictions.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
The governing law and dispute forum are clear and commercially reasonable.

### Medium
The jurisdiction is unusual or less convenient but remains understandable and
negotiable.

### High
The governing law creates meaningful legal or commercial exposure or lacks
important supporting dispute-resolution protections.

### Critical
The governing law is explicitly unacceptable, ambiguous, or combined with
other provisions in a way that creates severe legal exposure.

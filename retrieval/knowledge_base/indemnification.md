# Indemnification Playbook

## Fair Position

Indemnification obligations should be mutual where appropriate and limited to
claims arising from the indemnifying party's defined responsibilities.

The indemnifying party should not accept unlimited indemnification exposure
without a clear contractual cap or defined scope.

## Preferred Position

- Mutual indemnification where commercially appropriate.
- Clear definition of covered claims.
- Exclusions for the indemnitee's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach.
- Liability should remain subject to the agreed contractual cap unless a specific
  exception is expressly justified.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Indemnification is clearly scoped, commercially balanced, and subject to an
appropriate liability cap.

### Medium
The indemnification obligation is broader than preferred but remains bounded
and negotiable.

### High
Indemnification is materially one-sided, broadly worded, or exceeds the
playbook's acceptable exposure.

### Critical
Indemnification is unlimited or potentially unbounded, especially when combined
with another major liability exposure.

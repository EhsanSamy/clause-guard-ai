# Payment Terms Playbook

## Fair Position

Payment obligations should be clearly stated with reasonable payment periods,
defined invoicing requirements, and transparent consequences for late payment.

## Preferred Position

- Clear payment due dates.
- Clear invoicing requirements.
- Reasonable payment period.
- Transparent late-payment consequences.
- No unexpected or unlimited payment obligations.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Payment obligations are clear, bounded, and commercially reasonable.

### Medium
Payment terms are somewhat unfavorable or impose a longer-than-preferred
payment period but remain negotiable.

### High
Payment obligations create substantial unexpected financial exposure or impose
materially one-sided payment conditions.

### Critical
Payment obligations are potentially unlimited, undefined, or create severe
financial exposure without meaningful contractual protection.

# Auto Renewal Playbook

## Fair Position

Automatic renewal may be acceptable when both parties receive reasonable notice
and a practical opportunity to terminate before renewal.

The preferred opt-out notice period is approximately 30 to 45 days before renewal.

## Preferred Position

- Renewal terms should be clearly stated.
- Both parties should have a reasonable opt-out mechanism.
- Notice requirements should generally remain within 30–45 days.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Automatic renewal exists with a reasonable 30–45 day opt-out period.

### Medium
The opt-out period is longer than preferred but remains commercially common,
such as approximately 60 days.

### High
The renewal mechanism materially restricts the party's ability to opt out or
creates significant unexpected obligations.

### Critical
Renewal is effectively automatic with no practical termination mechanism or
creates severe, potentially unbounded obligations.

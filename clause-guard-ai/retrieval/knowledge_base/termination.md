# Termination Playbook

## Fair Position

Both parties should have clearly defined termination rights and reasonable
notice requirements.

Termination for convenience should generally be mutual or otherwise
commercially justified.

## Preferred Position

- Mutual termination rights where appropriate.
- Clear notice periods.
- Termination for cause for material breach.
- Reasonable opportunity to cure material breaches.
- No unilateral termination right that creates an unreasonable imbalance.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Termination rights are balanced and clearly defined.

### Medium
One provision is somewhat unfavorable but remains commercially negotiable.

### High
One party receives materially broader termination rights or the other party
loses an important standard protection.

### Critical
Termination rights are severely one-sided and create significant commercial
or legal exposure, particularly when combined with another major risk.

# Confidentiality Playbook

## Fair Position

Confidential information should be protected through clearly defined
confidentiality obligations with reasonable exclusions, permitted disclosures,
and duration.

## Preferred Position

- Clear definition of confidential information.
- Standard exclusions for publicly available or independently developed
  information.
- Reasonable permitted disclosures.
- Reasonable confidentiality duration.
- Balanced obligations where appropriate.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Confidentiality obligations are clear, balanced, and commercially reasonable.

### Medium
The obligations are broader or longer than preferred but remain negotiable.

### High
The clause imposes materially excessive confidentiality obligations or removes
important standard protections.

### Critical
The clause creates severe or potentially unlimited obligations with no practical
exceptions or protections.

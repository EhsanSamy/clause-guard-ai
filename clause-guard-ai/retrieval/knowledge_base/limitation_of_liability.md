# Limitation of Liability Playbook

## Fair Position

The agreement should contain a reasonable aggregate liability cap tied to the
commercial value of the contract.

A cap equal to approximately 12 months of fees is an acceptable fair position.

## Preferred Position

- Aggregate liability should be capped.
- The cap should be clearly stated.
- The cap should generally be tied to fees paid or payable under the agreement.
- Exceptions to the cap should be narrow and expressly justified.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Liability is capped at approximately 12 months of fees or a comparable
commercially reasonable amount.

### Medium
The liability cap is below the preferred position but remains bounded and
negotiable, or contains broader-than-preferred exceptions.

### High
The liability cap is materially above the fair position or important protections
are removed.

### Critical
Liability is uncapped or potentially unlimited without a justified and narrowly
defined exception.

# IP Assignment Playbook

## Fair Position

Intellectual property assignment should clearly identify the IP being assigned
and should generally be limited to work product created specifically under the
agreement.

Pre-existing intellectual property should remain with its original owner.

## Preferred Position

- Clearly defined assigned IP.
- Assignment limited to contract-related work product.
- Protection of pre-existing IP.
- Clear treatment of third-party materials.
- No unnecessary transfer of unrelated intellectual property.

## Risk Indicators

### Low
Assignment is clearly limited to relevant work product and preserves
pre-existing IP.

### Medium
The assignment is broader than preferred but remains commercially negotiable.

### High
The clause transfers substantial pre-existing or unrelated IP or removes
important ownership protections.

### Critical
The clause creates an effectively unlimited transfer of intellectual property
rights or claims ownership over unrelated assets without meaningful protection.
